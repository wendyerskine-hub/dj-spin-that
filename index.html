Make your own with Canva

Design one card, then print 9 on a page or order them

Fastest way: skip Canva. Use the photocard maker above with “Your own photo”, then tap Print sheets (9 per page). You get a page of 9 fronts and a page of 9 backs with cut marks, ready to print. Use Canva when you want your own layout or different photos on each card.

1 · Design one card

  1. Start a design. In Canva, tap Create a design, then Custom size. Pick mm and enter 55 × 85 (standard K-pop photocard size).
  2. Turn on print bleed (File, then Settings, then Show print bleed) and stretch your photo past the edges so nothing white shows after cutting.
  3. Add your photo and text. Use your own concert photos, or upload a front you made above. Keep names and words at least 3 mm in from the edge.
  4. Make the back on page 2: your handle, the date and city, or a little message.
  5. Save each side as a picture: Share, then Download, File type PNG, all pages. You get one image for the front and one for the back. You'll use these in step 2.

2 · Print 9 cards on one page at home

  1. Make a page-size design. Create a design, Custom size, mm: 215.9 × 279.4 for US Letter, or 210 × 297 for A4.
  2. Add your front. Uploads, then upload the front PNG from step 1 and tap it to put it on the page.
  3. Set its exact size. With the card selected, tap Position in the top bar. Under Advanced, type width 55 and height 85 (turn the lock off if it changes the other number).
  4. Make 9 and line them up. Duplicate the card (Ctrl+D on a computer, or the duplicate button on a phone) until you have 9. Give each one these X and Y numbers in the same Advanced box. Cards sit edge to edge, so one cut separates two cards.
    CardUS LetterA4
    Left columnX 25.45X 22.5
    Middle columnX 80.45X 77.5
    Right columnX 135.45X 132.5
    Top rowY 12.2Y 21
    Middle rowY 97.2Y 106
    Bottom rowY 182.2Y 191
  5. Make the backs. Add a second page and repeat with the back PNG, using the same numbers. If every back is the same, you're done. If the backs are different, swap the left and right columns, because the page flips over.
  6. Download: Share, Download, PDF Print. No crop marks needed for this layout.
  7. Print the fronts (page 1) on 250–300 gsm cardstock or photo paper. In the print box choose Actual size / 100%, not “Fit to page”, and the best quality setting.
  8. Print the backs. Put the printed sheet back in the tray flipped over the long edge and print page 2. Try it on plain paper first and hold it up to a light to check the backs line up. Printing two sheets and gluing them back to back works too.
  9. Cut and finish: cut along the card edges with a paper trimmer (4 cuts each way), round the corners with a 3–4 mm corner punch, and sleeve or laminate them to make them last.

3 · Or have them printed

  1. At a print shop (Staples, FedEx Office or a local shop): bring or upload the 9-per-page PDF from section 2 (or the print sheets from the maker above). Ask for double-sided on 100 lb cover cardstock (about 270 gsm), printed at actual size, and ask them to cut the cards for you. Most shops can do it the same day for a small cutting fee.
  2. From an online card printer: they usually want one card per file. In your 55 × 85 mm Canva design, Download, PDF Print, and tick Crop marks and bleed. Upload the front and back where the site asks for them.
  3. Pick the right size. Choose a photocard or custom size of 55 × 85 mm (2.17 × 3.35 in). Regular business cards (3.5 × 2 in) are a different shape and will crop your design.
  4. Options to ask for: 300–350 gsm card, matte or gloss, and rounded corners (about 3 mm). Order a small test batch first to check the colors.

Please don't sell cards made from official BTS photos or BT21 characters. Those belong to HYBE and LINE FRIENDS. Free sharing and trading between ARMY is the spirit here.

Coming soon: trusted ARMY trading groups.
  • Trade by mail? Use tracked shipping, and send to a P.O. box or pickup point if you can.
  • Never post your home address, phone number or full name in a public group.
  • Trading in person works best at the venue or in a busy public place, with a friend.
  • Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before trading with someone you don't know.
  • Check reviews or proof of past trades before sending first.
